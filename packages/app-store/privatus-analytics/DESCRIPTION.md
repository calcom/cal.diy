Privatus Analytics shows you how people find and use your booking pages. It uses no cookies.

Many analytics tools use cookies to follow people from site to site. Privatus Analytics counts visits without cookies and without storing who your visitors are. You still see where visitors come from, which pages they view and which links bring them to you.

To use it, add your site ID (it starts with pa_) to an event type. Privatus Analytics then adds its small tracking script to that booking page.
