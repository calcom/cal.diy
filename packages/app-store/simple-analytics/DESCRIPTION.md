Simple Analytics is a European, privacy-friendly and simple alternative to Google Analytics. No cookies, no trackers and no consent required from your visitors.

Use the Simple Analytics app to see how your booking pages perform:

- Page views of your booking pages, next to the rest of your website traffic
- An event when a booking is completed, rescheduled or cancelled

Setup: add the hostname of your website to your Simple Analytics dashboard, install this app and enter that hostname in the Apps tab of an event type. Page views and booking events are then recorded under that hostname.
