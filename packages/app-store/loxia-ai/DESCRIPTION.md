{DESCRIPTION}

[Loxia AI](https://loxiaai.com) enables businesses to automate inbound and outbound phone calls, web chat, and WhatsApp conversations with human-like multilingual AI agents. By connecting [Cal.com](https://cal.com) with Loxia AI, your AI receptionists can check real-time calendar availability and book confirmed appointments directly during live calls and chats — 24/7, in 13+ languages.

### Key Capabilities

- **Real-time Slot Lookup**: Loxia AI checks live availability on your Cal.com event types during phone calls, web chat, and WhatsApp conversations.
- **Instant Booking Creation**: Collects attendee name, email, time zone, and notes conversationally and creates confirmed bookings via the Cal.com API v2.
- **Omnichannel Coverage**: Works across AI Phone Agents, Unified Web Voice & Chat Widgets, and WhatsApp Business.
- **Zero Missed Leads**: Qualifies callers after hours, handles rescheduling inquiries, and syncs booking details directly into your Cal.com calendar.

### How to Connect Cal.com with Loxia AI

1. Sign in to your [Loxia AI Dashboard](https://loxiaai.com/en/dashboard/integrations) and select **Cal.com** under Calendar Integrations.
2. In your Cal.com account, go to **Settings → Developer → API Keys** and create a new API key (`cal_live_...`).
3. Paste your API key into Loxia AI (optionally specify a default Event Type ID and Time Zone) and click **Save**.
4. Your AI Voice & Chat agents can now automatically check open slots and book meetings on your Cal.com calendar.
